# Brand Spec — Dark Editorial Portfolio

## Visual Identity
A cinematic, high-end editorial direction adapted for a low-light creator portfolio.

### Palette (OKLch)
- `--bg`:      `oklch(14% 0.01 240)` (Deep Obsidian Ink)
- `--surface`: `oklch(18% 0.012 240)` (Slightly lighter surface)
- `--fg`:      `oklch(92% 0.005 240)` (Refined Paper White)
- `--muted`:   `oklch(58% 0.01 240)` (Muted Graphite)
- `--border`:  `oklch(22% 0.015 240)` (Ghostly Ink Border)
- `--accent`:  `oklch(68% 0.15 42)` (Aged Gold / Amber)

### Typography
- **Display**: `var(--font-playfair)`, Serif. Tight tracking (-0.03em), high contrast.
- **Body**: `var(--font-geist-sans)`, Sans. Clean, high readability.
- **Metadata/UI**: `var(--font-space-grotesk)`, Geometric Sans. Wide tracking for labels.

### Layout Posture
- **Editorial Rhythm**: Asymmetric grids, large serif headlines, generous but controlled whitespace.
- **Borders**: Hairline (0.5px - 1px) borders to define space without clutter.
- **Radii**: Soft but professional (12px - 24px).

## Motion & Reactivity
- **Music Sync**: "High" intensity. Background orbs, typography scale, border glow, and even layout transitions should pulse with the bass.
- **Smoothness**: Lenis smooth scroll + Framer Motion transitions.
- **Cinematic**: Film grain (SVG noise), ambient glows, and perspective shifts on hover.
